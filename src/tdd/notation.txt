The spec is caveman-encoded. Symbols are load-bearing:

→   leads to / becomes / triggers
∴   therefore / fix
∀   for all / every
∃   exists / some
!   must
?   may / optional
⊥   never / impossible / forbidden
≠   not equal / differs from
∈   in / member of
∉   not in
≤   at most
≥   at least
&   and
|   or

Sections: §G goal · §C constraints · §I interfaces · §R research · §V invariants (numbered, testable, each MUST hold) · §T tasks · §B bugs.
`§F` is a federation table: `dir|owns|⊥owns|tokens`, where `dir` is a CHILD DIRECTORY exactly one level below the node declaring it.
